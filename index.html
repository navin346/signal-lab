Watcher: what it has learned

The watcher copies the four bots into its own $500 account (a 25% share each) and learns which bot wins in which conditions. It starts as an equal split and tilts each copy between 0.5x and 1.5x only as evidence piles up. Conditions are only trusted after 30+ trades; "edge" is the extra profit (in R, 1R ≈ $5 = 1% risk) vs. the bot's average. Started .

    What the bots see right now

    Structure (market-structure bot): ▲ bullish / ▼ bearish after a close beyond a deep swing; the protected level is the higher low (or lower high) whose loss flips the trend; leg = the previous swing high/low it targets. Regime (news bot): ADX ≥ 25 trend, < 20 range. News: the analyst's time-decayed view, −1 to +1; the news bot needs ±0.20 to act, and the structure bot skips trades against ≤ −0.50.
